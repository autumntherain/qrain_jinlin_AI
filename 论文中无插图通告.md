# 论文无插图通告

各位用户、开发者：

特此通告：受论文原版插图版权约束，同时为避免重绘带来的工作量与潜在偏差，本项目附带的论文文档**不包含任何原文图表、插图**。

# No‑Illustration Notice

Dear users and developers,

Notice: Due to copyright restrictions on original paper figures and the risk of deviation introduced by redrawing, all paper documents provided in this project **do not include any original figures or illustrations**.
Please refer to the corresponding publicly‑available original papers for diagrams. Thank you for your understanding.
